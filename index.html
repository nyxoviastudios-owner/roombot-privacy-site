ROOMBOT

ROOMBOT is a personal project for preparing room-inspiration and home-decor Pinterest content around curated products. Draft content is intended to be reviewed by the operator before publication.

What ROOMBOT does

The project is being developed to help organise product links and prepare Pinterest Pin copy for home and room decor ideas. API integrations and automated publishing are still subject to setup, access approval, and testing.
